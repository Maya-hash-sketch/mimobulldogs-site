Our approach

The best start possible.

We spare no cost or effort to ensure our puppies get the best start possible. It starts with healthy nutrition and continues with early neurological stimulation, early (safe) socialization, and more. We continuously work on all recommended health testing for English Bulldogs.

🥩

Healthy Nutrition

We follow the guidelines of Juliette de Bairacli Levy, with a slow, natural weaning schedule onto a raw diet. A puppy's first eight weeks set the stage for a lifetime of health — raw-weaned puppies grow up strong, lively, and calm.

🧠

Early Neurological Stimulation

Gentle ENS exercises from day 3–16 to build resilience and stress tolerance — proven to produce calmer, more adaptable adult dogs.

🐾

Safe Socialization

Puppies are raised inside our home with exposure to daily family routines, household sounds, kids, and other dogs — confident, not shy, from day one.

💉

Vet Checked & Vaccinated

All puppies receive regular deworming, their first round of immunizations, and a thorough vet exam before leaving for their new homes.

📋

One-Year Health Guarantee

We offer a standard one-year health guarantee on every puppy. We stand behind every dog we breed.

🩺

Health Testing

We continuously work on all recommended health testing for English Bulldogs — because healthy parents make healthy puppies.

On raw feeding
"A puppy's first eight weeks set the stage for a lifetime of health or illness, so it isn't surprising that puppies weaned on raw food grow up to out-perform dogs weaned on kibble or canned food, even if both are fed raw food as adults. Raw-weaned puppies nearly exhaust their breeders' vocabularies, for these are the healthiest, strongest, liveliest, calmest, smartest, most wonderful pups that ever lived."

— Juliette de Bairacli Levy, via Whole Dog Journal
