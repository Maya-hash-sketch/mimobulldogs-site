Health & Training

Healthy Bulldogs start before they're born.

We breed for functional, breathing-easy Bulldogs — and that starts with raising puppies with a proven socialization program and sending them home ready to thrive.

The first 8 weeks

How we raise every litter.

From the day they're born, our puppies get intentional socialization so they grow up confident — not shy.

👃

Early Neurological Stimulation

Gentle ENS exercises from day 3–16 to build resilience and stress tolerance — proven to produce calmer, more adaptable adult dogs.

🥩

Healthy Whole-Food Nutrition

We follow a natural, raw-food approach to nutrition — because what a puppy eats in those first weeks shapes a lifetime of health. Healthy parents and healthy puppies start with real, whole food.

🫁

Agile Parents, Healthy Puppies

The foundation of a healthy puppy is healthy, agile parents with a long-term health history behind them. We breed Bulldogs who can move freely, breathe easily, and stay sound — and that starts with choosing parents whose health we know, not just how they look.

🚽

Potty Training Started

We start litter box / potty pad training before puppies go home, so you have a head start on the hardest part.

👨‍👩‍👧

Kid-Tested

Our puppies grow up around our family — so they're used to the volume, energy, and love that comes with kids before they come to yours.

🩺

Vet Checked

Every puppy sees our vet before going home — vaccinations, deworming, and a full wellness check on record.
