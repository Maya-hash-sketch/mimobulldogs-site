About MimoBulldogs

A small home-based breeder in Southern Indiana.

We've been raising AKC English Bulldogs with love since 2016. What started as one Bulldog we adored became a careful, heart-led breeding program focused on health, temperament, and family.

Our story

It started with one Bulldog we couldn't imagine life without.

MimoBulldogs began the way a lot of good things do — with one dog who changed everything for us. We fell in love with the breed's wrinkles, the snores, the stubborn charm, and the way a Bulldog becomes the heart of a home.

But we also saw how much the breed needed people who'd breed for health first. So in 2016, we started Mini Monster Bulldogs LLC — a small, home-based program in Southern Indiana focused on raising Bulldogs who can breathe freely, move comfortably, and live long, joyful lives.

We're not a kennel. Our dogs live in our house, sleep on our couches, and are part of our daily life. Puppies are raised underfoot — with kids, household sounds, and all the gentle chaos of a real home — so they arrive with you confident, social, and ready to be part of your family.

What we believe

Our approach in three words: health, home, heart.

Everything we do as a breeder comes back to these three things.

🫁

Health first

We health-test our breeding dogs and select pairs that produce puppies who breathe freely, move well, and stay sound. Functional Bulldogs, not just pretty ones.

🏠

Raised in our home

No kennels. Puppies grow up in our living room, meeting kids, other dogs, and everyday life. They go home socialized, confident, and used to a real household.

❤️

Lifelong heart

We don't disappear after you take a puppy home. We're here for questions, advice, and support for the life of your dog — because that's what responsible breeding means to us.
